01 · OVERVIEW

About
the Project.

Nurture explores how digital design can support couples in their daily lives after welcoming a child. The concept focuses on helping partners communicate, share responsibilities, and stay mindful of each other's need for rest and recovery.

Challenge

How might we help working parents and nannies stay connected and coordinated, ensuring their child's daily needs, safety, literacy, and development are consistently supported even when parents are away?

02 PROJECT FOCUS

From Concept
to Solution.

01

Mood & Communication

Mood check-ins and short reflections to help partners understand each other's feelings and needs.

02

Shared Responsibilities

Activity scheduling and task sharing to help partners plan and manage their daily routines together.

03

Room for Recovery

Recovery windows and mindful rest routines as part of the couple's daily life.
