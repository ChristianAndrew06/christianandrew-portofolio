01 · OVERVIEW

About
the Project.

Growey* is a digital wellbeing platform designed to help parents and caregivers stay connected, coordinate daily care, and support a child's wellbeing and development.

Challenge

How might we help working parents and nannies stay connected and coordinated to ensure their child's daily needs, safety, literacy, and development are supported even when parents are away?
